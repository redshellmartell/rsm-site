I made some changes to my website recently, and would like to share the what and why!

Did you notice my website has a new look? If you haven't been here in a while then its a good time to check it out! I decided its time to put some more focus here...I hope it is for the better! 😊

The Theme: Right away you can see a difference in the colors and font. I decided to spice things up a bit! Actually, I went a little less extreme. The black is more like a really dark gray, and the red is a lot less 'in your face.' For my color buffs out there, I went from a Crimson to a Cadmium Red Light. The biggest advantage of this theme is how it affects the blog here. Have you noticed? The text is not in italics. I tried so hard to change that in the past but I didn't realize it was all connected to my website theme. I am sorry for all of you who are annoyed by full italic text like me....I have now corrected that situation.

The Front Page: I used to have a small section for Aquaponics, which I included when I first created the site. It is one of my interests and I was really proud of what I made. However, it is not something I am actively doing at the moment. Therefore you will only find a Game Design and Artwork section now. If you like aquaponics, or want to learn more, you can always reach out to me anyway. I can talk enough about it to burn off both of your ears 😄 I am sticking to Art and Game design these days, so I think it is most appropriate to keep here. Do you think there is anything missing from the front page? If so I would love to hear your feedback!!

The Members Area/Shop: Something I noticed....everybody has a shop, and a place to create a membership. Not that I want to be like everyone else....but ya know, those things seem important😅 Actually, it was quite an experience to create the shop and connect my business and bank to it. I am glad I took the time to learn. Also who knows, maybe I will even add some products there eventually 😋 If you create an account then you can get a notification when I make a blog post and/or you can interact with me here and leave some comments. (Thanks Albert!)

The Newsletter Archive: My Favorite Part!!!!! I added a section under my contact page to include links to all of my past Newsletters. I know that some of you have signed up recently so here is a chance to read the backlog.....of course you must be pretty bored to do that, ha!! I feel blessed when somebody signs up for my newsletter, so that I something I am always looking forward to writing.

Anyway, I hope you enjoyed some insight into the changes I made to the RSM Productions website. I would love to hear your feedback about it. Do you like it? Is there anything I am missing? What do you want to see more of? This site is a way to for me to connect with you, and I am here to serve 😊

Until next time!
