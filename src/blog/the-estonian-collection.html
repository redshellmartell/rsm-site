Hey Everyone! I was thinking to myself the other day, how long have I been an artist? It is easy to say that I have been making art my whole life, starting out as a kid like many of us do :) It has been 9 years since I took a professional interest in art. I couldn't believe it!

Far too long without a website and a blog.

I have come a long way and hope to share my journey with you. In my first series of blog posts I will be highlighting my Estonian Collection. As many of you know, I have been living in Estonia for almost 4 years now. During this time I have grown a lot professionally and artistically.

It's safe to say that the Estonian Collection is the culmination of my experience as an artist.

You will get a chance to see these paintings up close and personal through my blog. I will share my impressions and give some insight into what I was thinking at the time. Hopefully we can spark some conversation as well! I am really focusing on my best work, and will also show some honorable mentions - some of the paintings that helped me gain experience along the way.

Stay tuned!
